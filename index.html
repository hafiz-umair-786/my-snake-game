How to play

Guide the coil through the grid and collect energy cells. Green cells add 1 point; red poison cells remove 3 points, even below zero. Yellow obstacles move to new positions every 10 seconds. Touch the directional pad on mobile. You have 3 lives; collisions use one life and respawn you at a safe position.
